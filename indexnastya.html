Project Description: Restaurant Branding

A deep dive into the branding process for "The Golden Ladle," a modern eatery focused on fresh, local ingredients. This project showcases the journey from initial concept to a full-fledged brand identity.

Step 1: Conceptualization & Mood Boards. Our journey began with exploring various visual directions to capture the restaurant's unique ambiance of rustic elegance. We focused on typography, color palettes, and initial logo sketches to define the core feeling of the brand.

Step 2: Logo & Visual Identity. We refined the chosen concept, developing a versatile logo system. This stage involved creating digital mockups to see the brand in context on signage, menus, and staff uniforms, ensuring consistency across all touchpoints.

Step 3: Print Collateral & Packaging. The menu is a key brand touchpoint. We designed a clean, readable menu and extended the brand identity to takeout bags, cups, and other packaging to create a cohesive off-premise experience.

Step 4: Interior & Environmental Graphics. The brand was seamlessly integrated into the physical space. We designed wall graphics, wayfinding signage, and other decorative elements that reinforce the brand story and create an immersive atmosphere for guests.

Step 5: Digital Presence. To bring the brand online, we designed a responsive website and created a suite of social media templates. This ensures a consistent and engaging visual style for attracting new guests and communicating with loyal customers.

My Journey

From Curiosity to Creation

My path to UX design wasn't a straight line, but a journey fueled by a fascination with how people think and interact with the world. With a degree in Cognitive Science, I started my career in research, but I always felt a pull towards a more creative application of my insights. That's when I discovered the world of UX, a perfect blend of empathy, analysis, and artistry.

Today, I channel that passion into designing digital products. Outside of work, I'm an avid hiker, a hobbyist photographer, and I believe that the best ideas often come when you're unplugged and exploring the world around you. I bring this perspective of balance and curiosity to every project I take on.
